How to use mod .ZIP?

Setup

I will be covering how to do this with the R2modman mod manager. Thunderstore Mod Manager should be pretty similar but may vary slightly.

Step 1

Go to settings tab in R2modman. Make sure you are in the "All" settings category.

Step 2

Search for "Local". Click the "Import local mod" button/setting.

Step 3

Simply select your .ZIP file! It should now be loaded in R2modman/TSMM!

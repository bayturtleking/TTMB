About TTMB

Hi, I'm "Turtle", the creator and primary maintainer of Turtle's TABS Mod Browser (TTMB).

Why is TTMB a thing?

I made TTMB because of a community complaint about the Thunderstore site: we can not control it. There were many mods deemed low-quality that kept being published and we had no control over it. So, this was made as a small solution: we don't have to stop these mods from being published. Instead, we can give the user control over what mods they want to see. Since then, the site and idea has grown into what TTMB is now: a user-orientated, privacy-safe mod browser giving only what you want to see!

How the filters work

TTMB uses a mix of automatic detection and manual review & filter lists.. None of our filters are perfect, and we understand that. That is why the TABS modding community is reached out to for almost every decision made, regardless of size.

Where do the mods come from?

TTMB uses Thunderstore API data to show all the same mods Thunderstore.io does (including my mods!) but with a more privacy-safe (no tracking or accounts), customizable, mod browsing experience with filters and categorized mods. We also have a few "TTMB Exclusive" mods, which are mods only available on TTMB because of certain situations such as not being permitted on other platforms despite being useful and harmless.

Help the Community!

I maintain TTMB as a one man project, but the GitHub is open! You can make contributions and make it better yourself. You can suggest improvements or contribute to the site on the TTMB GitHub repo.

The filter lists and automatic detection blacklists/whitelists are in a separate repo, so almost anyone can help update the allowlists, blocklists, and manually curated filters without needing to touch any code. (JSON isn't really code, right?)

Appealing Filter for mod

If you think a mod you made has been negatively impacted by TTMB or put into incorrect categories, filters, or blacklists, use the GitHub issue templates to explain it and appeal the filters.
